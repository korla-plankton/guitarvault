## Full Description

```
GuitarVault is the collection manager for guitar enthusiasts. Catalogue every instrument with deep specifications, capture photos with on-device AI background removal, track valuations and insurance, and fill in your knowledge one spec at a time with the Random Spec Challenge.

🎸 DEEP SPECIFICATIONS
60+ fields per guitar: body, neck and fretboard woods, scale length in decimal inches, neck profile, fret size, pickups, electronics, hardware, tuning machines, finish, serial number, strings, case and accessories — plus a proper body construction setting (solid, chambered, semi-hollow, hollow). Don't see a field you need? Add your own custom fields.

📸 PHOTO CAPTURE + AI
Take photos with the built-in camera (snappy capture, correct orientation every time), paste reference images from your browser, or pick from your gallery. The magic wand runs ML Kit on-device AI to remove backgrounds — with undo if it doesn't get it right. Tag and caption photos, set a primary shot, and browse full-screen with pinch-to-zoom and swipe between photos.

💰 VALUATION & INSURANCE
Track purchase price, date and source, current value with full history, and estimated value — see gain or loss at a glance. Record insurance details: provider, policy number, coverage type, deductible. The dashboard warns you when your insured coverage falls short of your collection's value.

🔧 CONDITION & MAINTENANCE
Rate condition from Mint to Poor, with a dated history log. Log every string change, setup, repair, refret and electronics work with date performed, cost and technician — editable and deletable, so historical maintenance is easy to backfill.

🎲 RANDOM SPEC CHALLENGE
The app picks a random guitar from any list — owned, wishlist or sold — shows its photo, and asks you to fill in one missing spec. Stuck? One tap opens a targeted search for exactly that spec. Run it as often as you like and watch your collection completeness grow.

🔍 SPEC SEARCH
Search Google for specs or images, or browse Reverb and eBay listings — all pre-filled with your guitar's details.

📦 BACKUP & TRANSFER
Export your entire collection, photos included, as a single ZIP file. Move to a new phone or share a backup, then import with a choice: merge into your existing collection (matching guitars combine, nothing is deleted) or replace it entirely. Every import asks for confirmation first.

📋 WISHLIST, OWNED & SOLD
Unified status tabs with live counts let you manage guitars you own, want, and have sold. Promote a wishlist guitar to owned with one tap.

🎨 YOUR WAY
Material You dynamic colour with a theme selector — follow the system, or lock light or dark mode.

🔒 100% PRIVATE
All data stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics, no ads, no tracking — Android's automatic cloud backup is disabled too. Your collection never leaves your phone unless you export it yourself.

KEY FEATURES:
• 60+ specification fields plus custom user-defined fields
• Solid / chambered / semi-hollow / hollow body construction tracking
• Camera capture, clipboard paste, and gallery import for photos
• On-device AI background removal with undo
• Photo tags, captions and primary photo selection
• Full-screen viewer with pinch-zoom and swipe
• Valuation history, insurance tracking and coverage gap warning
• Condition ratings and editable maintenance log with dates
• Random Spec Challenge gamification with targeted lookup
• Search Google (specs & images), Reverb and eBay
• Owned / Wishlist / Sold tabs with counts
• ZIP backup export with photos; merge or replace import
• List, grouped and grid views; sort by name, brand, year, value, purchase date
• Light / dark / system theme with Material You colour
• 100% local and private — no cloud, no account, no ads
```
